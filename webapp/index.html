Road Surface Damage Taxonomy

Four primary distress classes formalized by the Global Road Damage Detection Challenge.

D00

Longitudinal Crack

Linear fissures extending parallel to the pavement centerline, typically caused by joint settlement or wheel path fatigue.

D10

Transverse Crack

Cracks running perpendicular to the pavement traffic direction, typically caused by temperature fluctuations or subgrade shrinkage.

D20

Alligator Crack

Interconnected pattern of polygon fissures resembling reptile skin, indicating structural failure in base support layers.

D40

Pothole

Bowl-shaped pavement depressions caused by water intrusion into underlying aggregate followed by freeze-thaw cycles and heavy load impact.
