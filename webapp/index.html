Standardized Distress Definitions

Road Surface Damage Taxonomy

Four primary damage categories formalized by the IEEE / Global Road Damage Detection Challenge.

D00

Longitudinal Crack

Linear fissures extending parallel to the pavement centerline, typically caused by joint settlement, improper construction seams, or wheel path fatigue.

D10

Transverse Crack

Cracks running perpendicular to the pavement traffic direction, typically caused by low temperature contraction or reflection cracking from sub-base layers.

D20

Alligator Crack

Interconnected series of polygon fissures resembling reptile skin, indicating critical structural failure in base support layers under repeated heavy axle loads.

D40

Pothole

Bowl-shaped pavement depressions caused by water penetration into underlying base material followed by freeze-thaw erosion and localized structural collapse.
